A LITTLE LETTER

For you,
always ♡

♡

Dear Jas,

Eversince college until now, ikaw na yung isa sa mga taong halos kasama ko sa lahat. Ang dami na nating pinagdaanan, and somehow, hanggang ngayon nandito pa rin tayo. Alam mo na halos lahat tungkol sa akin — yung mga kwento ko, mga problema, mga drama, pati yung mga bagay na minsan hindi ko na kayang ikwento sa iba.

Thank you for always listening to me, kahit paulit-ulit na minsan yung mga kwento ko. Thank you for being there whenever I need someone to talk to, and for simply being there kahit wala naman tayong kailangang gawin. Yung presence mo pa lang, malaking bagay na.

I'm really, really lucky to have you as my best friend. And on your birthday, ang pinaka-wish ko talaga for you is sana maging genuinely happy ka palagi. Sana makuha mo lahat ng deserve mo — peace of mind, good health, success, happiness, and syempre... sana yumaman na tayo. HAHAHA.

I'm looking forward to all the years and memories we're still going to make together. I hope kahit gaano pa magbago yung buhay natin, we'll still find our way back to each other and continue being part of each other's lives.

And please remember na nandito lang ako. Kapag kailangan mo ng kausap, kasama, makikinig sa problema, taga-rant, taga-support, or kahit taga-aya lang kumain, you know where to find me. Hindi man ako laging may tamang advice, at lalong hindi ako laging may pera para ilibre ka HAHAHA, pero I'll always be here for you in whatever way I can.

Sorry, wala munang bonggang birthday gift na pera. Mahirap din ako ngayon, beh. 😭😂 Pero sana balang araw, pareho na tayong mayaman at wala nang iniisip na bills. Manifesting!

For now, I just hope you know how much you mean to me and how grateful I am that I get to call you my bestie.

Happy 28th Birthday, Jas! ♡

I love you, and I'm always rooting for you.

Here's to more years, more memories, more food trips, more kwentos, more laughs, and more life together.

Always,
Your Bestie ♡
